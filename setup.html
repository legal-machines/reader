Read encrypted mail in this browser

Your key file is opened here, in this browser, and nothing leaves it: this page cannot send anything anywhere. The key is then locked to this device with a passkey (Touch ID, a fingerprint or the screen lock) and a PIN you choose, and kept only in this browser.

Keys in this browser

    Add a key

    Your key came from your mailbox, locked with its passphrase. Enter the passphrase to add it to this browser.

    Messages open with Touch ID (or your device's screen lock). A PIN adds a second step: without it the key does not open, even for someone who knows your computer's password.
