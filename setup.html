Add a key

Your key came from your mailbox, locked with its passphrase. Enter the passphrase to add it to this browser.

Messages open with Touch ID, your fingerprint or the screen lock. A PIN adds a second step: without it the key does not open, even for someone who knows your computer's password.

Your key in Thunderbird or another mail app

Thunderbird, GnuPG and most other mail apps open a key locked the classic way. Choose the file encryption-key.asc from the message "Your encryption key" in your mailbox and type the recovery code from your sheet: Seal saves a copy locked with the same code, which those apps open. Nothing leaves this browser.

Saved. In Thunderbird: Account Settings, End-To-End Encryption, Add Key, Import an existing OpenPGP Key. Choose the copy and type the recovery code.

Connect your key to Mail

Mail has not taken your key yet, so it does not open your encrypted messages in this browser. Connect it: a tab of Mail opens, and your key is kept there once you press Connect.
